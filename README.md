# 🎬 MyDairy

**Watch • Track • Remember**

A simple, stylish diary for your movies, series and anime. Track what you're watching, rate what you've finished, and get picks from **Jarvis AI**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎞️ Movies, TV shows and anime in one place
- ⏯️ Continue watching with progress tracking
- ⭐ Ratings, favourites and watch history
- 📚 Watchlist, genre playlists and statistics
- 🤖 Jarvis AI for recommendations and quick answers
- 📱 Installable PWA with offline support
- 🎨 Dark / light theme and 5 accent colours
